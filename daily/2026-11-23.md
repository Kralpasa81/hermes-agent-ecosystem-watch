## Broader AI agent ecosystem
- **OpenAI's Chief Scientist proposes perplexity as a shared pacing metric (Nov 22).** In "Perplexity and Intelligence," Jakub Pachocki publishes OpenAI's internal log-perplexity curve (GPT-3.5 → GPT-6.2 Astra) and argues for perplexity (cross-entropy loss) as a cross-lab standard for measuring pretraining progress and for setting **common limits** that developers agree not to cross until monitoring/alignment auditing matures. It also flags the ~0.68–0.7 log-perplexity window as the next capability threshold and notes AI has begun contributing to pretraining-algorithm research. A research/governance framing rather than an agent-tooling change; it slipped past the quiet 11-22 note. ([post](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/))
