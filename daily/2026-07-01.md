## Hermes / Hermes-adjacent

- **Main-branch browser/CDP token redaction hardening:** Hermes consolidated CDP-URL log redaction into a single chokepoint and closed remaining supervisor/session leak paths. This is a meaningful privacy/security signal for browser automation because CDP URLs can carry sensitive tokens. Sources: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/c626dde, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/265da9c, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/e09ff88
- **Cron reliability for scheduled jobs:** Hermes fixed null-safe delivery handling in cron listing and re-resolves BSM secrets per run, reducing stale-secret and listing-edge failures in recurring automation. Source: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/836732f
- **Context accounting fixes after compression:** Hermes clamped `-1` post-compression sentinels in TUI/gateway and sibling status paths, improving context-usage reporting reliability for long sessions after compression. Sources: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/b6d8fc4, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/8db6ed7
- **Agent safety and recovery polish:** Hermes now avoids persisting empty-response recovery scaffolding, restricts todo hydration to paired assistant todo calls, and bounds threat-pattern / FTS5 regex inputs while covering V4A Move-File edits. Sources: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/f2a528f, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/bc6cd46, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/060779b
