# 2026-08-08

## Hermes / Hermes-adjacent

### Mixture of Agents improvements
- **Advisor cadence change**: Default advisor cadence changed from `per_iteration` to `user_turn` in July 2026 update, significantly reducing per-turn latency and cost. Users can still opt for `per_iteration` or `every_n` modes when fresh per-tool iteration advice is needed.
- **Reference output capping**: Added `reference_max_tokens` configuration option to cap advisor output length, providing concise advice and improving turn speed without sacrificing answer quality.
- **Privacy filter**: Introduced privacy filter with `display` and `full` modes to redact sensitive information from advisor outputs in user-visible surfaces and traces.
