# 2026-11-07

## Hermes / Hermes-adjacent
- **Arete-27B — Nous Research post-trains Qwen3.8-27B to reinforce Western values (Nov 6).** Nous took Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B (the strongest performant local model they tested) and post-trained it to strip state-aligned framing while preserving capability. Headlines:
  - **Open eval, weights, and data.** `Propaganda_Eval` is 803 prompts across seven value categories (freedom of expression, state ideology, state vs. free market, education, data privacy & surveillance, democracy, environment), scored by an LLM judge on factual recall and propaganda framing. Weights (`NousResearch/Arete-27B`) and training data (`arete-data-pairs`, ~28,886 examples) are open on Hugging Face.
  - **Measured result.** Propaganda rate fell from 22% → 1%, and the fact score rose from 2.32 → 2.98 (out of 5); zero-scored answers dropped 359 → 216 of 803. The largest swing was freedom of expression (fact 0.89 → 2.44, propaganda 60% → 1%).
  - **No capability regression.** Hermes Bench 65.60% vs. base 65.77% (within noise); Terminal-Bench-2.1 70% vs. 67%.
  - **Method.** Fine-tuned ~52.8M tokens (~50k samples at 64k context) over 2 epochs / 1,202 steps, no RL or preference optimization, no architecture change; teacher model is the open Inkling. The takeaway Nous stresses: the value-bias layer in Chinese-aligned models is "shallow" and removable in ~50M tokens. ([blog](https://nousresearch.com/arete))
