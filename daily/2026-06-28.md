Sources: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/578e3989, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/b8e22686, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/2701ea2f, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/c946e670

Sources: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/c9df4bc0, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/ccf52696, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/fc70d023, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/c393a8e5, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/a7fd62d8, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/90d25adc

Sources: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/f3aaba7f, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/002357a8, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/a94f657a, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/54ea0599, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/8194dbf6, https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/commit/2d206a3a
